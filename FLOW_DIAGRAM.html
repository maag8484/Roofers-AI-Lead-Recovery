A — Lead Recovery (What Happens in the Field)
📞
Homeowner calls, texts, or submits a web form
A storm-damage inquiry, missed call, or after-hours contact triggers the system automatically. The roofing company doesn't need to do anything.
🤖
AI responds instantly (within 30 seconds)
The AI automation engine sends a personalized SMS, qualifies the homeowner, answers questions, and checks the roofer's available calendar slots — all automatically, 24/7.
📅
Inspection booked on the roofer's Google Calendar
The appointment lands directly on the roofing company's calendar. The customer dashboard updates in real time — no manual entry, no follow-up calls needed.
