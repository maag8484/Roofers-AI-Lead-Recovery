# Roof AI demand-generation execution package

Prepared October 6, 2026. This package is ready for account buildout. It does not authorize ad spend.

## Commercial objective

Acquire independent roofing-company trials that activate a verified missed-call workflow and become paying subscriptions. Optimize for paid customers and collected revenue, not clicks, impressions or raw leads.

## Channel order

1. High-intent Google Search
2. Retargeting after a sufficient first-party audience exists
3. Roofing-agency referrals
4. Organic search and short demonstrations
5. Meta prospecting only after the proof-first creative is ready
6. LinkedIn only if a higher-cost professional test is separately approved

## Proposed first paid test

- Duration: 14 days
- Recommended ceiling: $30/day, $420 maximum
- Status: **PAUSED — exact budget approval required**
- Geography: Florida, Texas, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Alabama, Arizona and Nevada
- Device and schedule: all devices; business-hours bid observation first, without unsupported scheduling assumptions
- Landing page: `/how-much-are-missed-calls-costing-your-roofing-company/`
- Primary conversion: confirmed audit request
- Secondary conversions: calculator completion, signup intent and verified trial activation
- Business outcome: first paid Stripe invoice, reported separately from a trial

Do not launch if the live page, form, analytics, checkout or current offer cannot be verified.

## Meta campaign package

### Creative 1 — “On the roof”

1. Roofer is working and the phone rings.
2. Screen shows “unanswered call” rather than a fake lost-job amount.
3. An approved response collects the property city and roofing need.
4. The office receives a callback request with a named owner.
5. End card: “Your team answers first. Roof AI covers the approved gaps.”

### Creative 2 — “After hours”

1. Evening call arrives after the office closes.
2. Caller identifies a leak or replacement need.
3. Workflow collects useful details without promising emergency service.
4. End card: “Give after-hours roofing calls a clear next step.”

### Creative 3 — “Audit the handoff”

1. Show missed calls, repeat calls and spam being separated.
2. Show legitimate new opportunities assigned to an owner.
3. Show appointment and paid job as separate, later outcomes.
4. End card: “Measure first. Automate the verified gap.”

Audience order:

1. Site visitors and calculator starters who did not submit an audit
2. Video viewers who watched at least half of a proof-first demonstration
3. Customer list or lookalike only after lawful, permissioned first-party data exists
4. Broad home-service business audience as a controlled prospecting test

Do not describe job-title or interest targeting as precise owner verification. Exclude converted customers and internal QA traffic.

## Partnership offer

Primary targets: roofing marketing agencies and consultants. Ask for one consenting roofing client whose marketing produces phone inquiries but whose busy or after-hours process is unverified.

Mutual benefit:

- agency protects the response path behind its lead-generation work;
- roofer receives a scoped workflow review and authorized test;
- Roof AI supplies a factual handled / qualified / next-step report;
- no integration, commission, exclusivity or paid placement is assumed.

## Automated funnel gates

1. Visitor starts calculator or audit.
2. Contact is created only after confirmed form acceptance.
3. Email or SMS nurturing requires the corresponding consent and a verified sender.
4. Qualified buyer receives the current demo or checkout link only after fit is established.
5. Checkout displays the exact trial end and first charge date.
6. Configuration review precedes activation.
7. One authorized end-to-end test must pass.
8. Usage reminders reflect actual events, not fabricated urgency.
9. Referral or review request occurs only after a verified customer outcome and permission.

## Decision rules

- Do not scale on click-through rate alone.
- Pause a keyword or creative after meaningful spend with no qualified audit, using the approved test budget as the boundary.
- Do not call a free trial a customer.
- Scale only after an acquisition path produces an independent paid Stripe invoice.
- Report customer acquisition cost from actual ad spend divided by independent paid customers.
- Track trial-to-paid, first-to-second invoice, refunds and collected revenue.
- If volume is too small for a fair decision, extend time before increasing budget.
