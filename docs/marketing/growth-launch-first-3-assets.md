### Final post copy

A missed roofing call is not automatically lost revenue. It is an opportunity with an unknown outcome.

A clean recovery process makes every stage visible:

**Missed call → two-way response → qualified opportunity → booked estimate → verified job**

Why keep the stages separate?

- A returned message does not prove the caller is qualified.
- A qualified caller does not yet have an appointment.
- A booked estimate is not a signed job.
- Estimated pipeline is not collected revenue.

Roof AI Lead Recovery is designed to help roofing companies give missed and after-hours callers a clear next step while preserving honest measurement.
