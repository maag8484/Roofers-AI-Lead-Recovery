# Roof AI: 10 Paid Sign-Ups per Month Funnel Plan

Prepared: September 4, 2026
Status: Internal operating plan only. No deployment, publishing, spending, prospect contact, partner contact, or outbound change is authorized by this document.

## Outcome and definition

The monthly target is **10 new paid roofing-company accounts**, defined as accounts that complete the 7-day trial and produce a successful first $299 subscription charge. At current published pricing, that equals **$2,990 in gross new monthly recurring revenue (MRR)** before fees, discounts, refunds, cancellations, and usage costs.

The positioning remains: Roof AI helps recover inbound opportunities a roofer already generates. A missed call is not automatically a lost lead or lost revenue, and no funnel stage guarantees a signed roofing job.

## Current launch inventory

- 15-page Revenue Recovery Hub, calculator, cornerstone guides, and resource library on the review branch.
- Audit-request form, calculator attribution, UTM/click-ID capture, and non-PII analytics events prepared on the review branch.
- 30 days of draft educational distribution content; none published.
- 100 researched roofing-company prospects across DFW, Charlotte, Jacksonville, and Columbus; none contacted.
- 10 researched agency, media, CRM, call-tracking, and industry partner opportunities; none contacted.
- Production launch, indexing, publishing, outreach, and paid acquisition remain gated by explicit approval.

## Base funnel model

These are planning assumptions, not observed conversion rates. Replace each assumption with measured cohort data after launch.

| Monthly stage | Target | Assumed conversion to next stage | Qualification rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Qualified roofing-company visits | 1,200 | 20% | A U.S. roofing-company visitor who engages with a high-intent page or campaign |
| Calculator completions | 240 | 12.5% | All four calculator inputs completed; do not send input values as personal analytics data |
| Qualified audit requests | 30 | 67% | Identifiable roofing company, valid work contact, service area, and plausible missed-call need |
| Completed audits/demos | 20 | 75% | Owner/operator attends and the current call workflow is reviewed |
| Trials started | 15 | 67% | Account reaches trial status and begins onboarding |
| New paid accounts | **10** | — | Trial produces a successful first $299 charge |

Base-case economics: **$2,990 gross new MRR per month** and **$35,880 annualized recurring revenue** if all 10 accounts remain active for 12 months. The annualized figure is a run-rate illustration, not contracted or guaranteed revenue.

### Weekly control targets

Use a rolling four-week month so shortfalls appear before month-end.

| Weekly stage | Target |
|---|---:|
| Qualified visits | 300 |
| Calculator completions | 60 |
| Qualified audit requests | 7–8 |
| Completed audits/demos | 5 |
| Trials started | 3–4 |
| New paid accounts | 2–3 |
| Gross new MRR | $598–$897 |

If qualified traffic is on target but audit requests are below 7 per week, improve the calculator-to-audit handoff before adding traffic. If audits are on target but trials are below 3 per week, improve the audit, demo, fit criteria, and offer. If trials are on target but paid conversions are below 2 per week, prioritize activation and trial follow-up.

## Channel contribution hypothesis

Do not force every channel to produce equal volume. The starting base-case split is:

| Channel | Paid-account target per month | Primary leading indicator |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified prospecting from the 100-company pilot | 4 | Positive replies and completed audits |
| Agency/referral/co-branded audit partners | 3 | Partner-introduced audits |
| Organic search and educational distribution | 2 | Non-branded qualified visits and audit requests |
| Customer referral or verified proof loop | 1 | Referred trial starts |

This split is an assumption. Reallocate effort monthly based on paid-account conversion, activation, retention, sales time, and attributable acquisition cost—not impressions or raw clicks.

## Activation and retention scorecard

Acquisition is not complete at account creation. Track cohorts by original source and recovery channel.

### Activation milestones

| Milestone | Initial target | Measurement window |
|---|---:|---|
| Trial account completes core business profile | 90% | Within 24 hours |
| Phone/missed-call routing and calendar connection completed | 80% | Within 48 hours |
| At least one realistic test scenario passes | 80% | Within 72 hours |
| Account is live with approved rules | 75% | By day 4 |
| First eligible missed call is handled | Report, do not target artificially | During trial |
| First two-way homeowner conversation | Report, do not guarantee | During first 30 days |
| First qualified opportunity or approved handoff | Report, do not guarantee | During first 30 days |
| First confirmed estimate attributable to recovery | Report separately from a signed job | During first 30 days |

### Retention measures

- Trial-to-first-paid conversion: **at least 67%** in the base model.
- 30-day paid logo retention: **at least 90%** as an initial operating target.
- Monthly paid logo churn after the first 30 days: **below 5%** as an initial target; report small-cohort counts alongside percentages.
- Time to live, configuration failures, unhandled exceptions, appointments confirmed, verified jobs, support response time, pause usage, cancellation reason, and reactivation rate.
- For every customer proof item, distinguish missed calls, two-way responses, qualified opportunities, booked estimates, signed jobs, collected revenue, and estimated pipeline.

## Experiment operating rules

For every approved test, record: experiment ID, date, audience/source, UTM campaign, number eligible, delivered/reached, replies or engaged visits, calculator completions, qualified audit requests, audits held, trials, paid accounts, gross new MRR, activation milestones, retained accounts, direct cost, staff time, and reason lost.

- Use one opportunity record across calls, texts, forms, audits, demos, and trials.
- Preserve the original acquisition source separately from the recovery or follow-up channel.
- Never credit one company twice.
- Judge early tests primarily on qualified audits, trials, paid accounts, activation, and retention; use traffic and engagement only as diagnostic measures.
- Pause a test when it attracts poor-fit companies, creates compliance ambiguity, overwhelms fulfillment, or fails its pre-agreed quality threshold.
- Do not claim statistical certainty from small cohorts; report counts and rates together.

## Approval gates

1. **Technical launch:** preview QA, analytics verification, production merge/deploy, sitemap submission, and indexing require explicit approval.
2. **External communication:** every first prospect/partner batch, sender identity, message, follow-up sequence, community post, media pitch, and customer contact requires explicit approval.
3. **Commercial terms:** discounts, referral fees, guarantees, refunds, trials, and partner economics require explicit approval.
4. **Spend:** no advertising, sponsorship, software purchase, list purchase, or paid placement without explicit budget approval.
5. **Claims and customer proof:** verify source records and obtain permission before publishing names, quotes, performance, revenue, or outcomes.
6. **Compliance and data:** review consent, disclosure, opt-out, recording, texting, privacy, retention, and partner data-sharing requirements for the actual workflow and jurisdictions.
