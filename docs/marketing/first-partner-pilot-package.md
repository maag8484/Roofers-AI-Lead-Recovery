## Pilot concept: The Roofing Missed-Call Opportunity Audit

### Contractor-facing promise

> Find the measurable response gap in the roofing demand you already generate—without assuming every missed call is a lost job.

This is a **measurement and workflow audit**, not a guaranteed-revenue offer. It separates raw calls from legitimate opportunities, identifies where follow-up breaks down and gives the contractor a prioritized recovery plan.

### Co-branded deliverables

1. A partner-specific landing path to the live missed-call calculator with tagged attribution.
2. A 30-minute intake covering call volume, business hours, service area, job types, capacity and current follow-up.
3. Review of one recent 30-day reporting period, using data the contractor is authorized to provide.
4. A concise scorecard showing:
   - total and unique missed callers;
   - new-opportunity candidates versus existing customers, spam, vendors and duplicates;
   - business-hours versus after-hours gaps;
   - current time to initial response, where available;
   - contacted, qualified, appointment-requested and confirmed-appointment counts;
   - estimated gross contract value at risk under clearly labeled contractor-supplied assumptions;
   - three recommended workflow changes.
5. Optional 30-day Roof AI test for contractors that qualify and explicitly opt in.
6. A post-pilot outcome summary suitable for the partner, using aggregated or approved data only.

## Measurement framework

### Definitions

- **Referred contractor:** a unique company that reaches the tagged pilot page or is introduced by the partner.
- **Qualified audit request:** a US roofing company with legitimate inbound call volume, an eligible service model, decision-maker participation and current capacity to accept work.
- **Completed audit:** the baseline is reviewed and the contractor receives the scorecard.
- **Activated test:** the approved missed-call workflow is configured and passes test scenarios.
- **Recovered conversation:** a unique eligible missed caller enters a two-way conversation after the recovery trigger.
- **Qualified recovered opportunity:** the caller meets the contractor's documented service-area and job-fit criteria.
- **Booked estimate:** a confirmed calendar appointment, not merely a requested time.
- **Verified paid customer:** a referred company begins a paid Roof AI subscription after the agreed trial or test.
- **Verified job value:** contract value later confirmed by the contractor; it remains separate from estimated pipeline and collected revenue.

### Attribution rules

1. Store the original demand source separately from the partner referral and Roof AI recovery channel.
2. Use one company record and one opportunity record to prevent duplicate credit.
3. Credit the partner for the account introduction for the agreed attribution window; do not credit the partner or Roof AI for the contractor's underlying lead source.
4. Report estimated pipeline, signed contract value, completed work and collected revenue as separate stages.
5. Replace assumptions with verified outcomes when the contractor supplies them.
6. Do not publish a case study, logo, quote or customer-level result without written permission.

### Pilot success gates

These are decision thresholds, not promised outcomes.

- At least 3 qualified agency-client audits accepted and 2 completed.
- At least 5 qualified editorial audit requests and 3 completed.
- At least 60% of completed audits advance to a fit decision within seven days.
- At least 2 contractor tests activate and pass workflow QA.
- Zero duplicate attribution, unauthorized communications or unsafe emergency-service promises.
- At least 1 paid account from the combined pilots, plus a documented reason for every non-conversion.
- Any expansion decision also reviews 30-day activation quality; referral volume alone is insufficient.

## Commercial guardrails

1. **Validation before economics.** The first limited pilot is no-fee and non-exclusive. No commission, sponsorship purchase or media spend is authorized at this stage.
2. **No open-ended free service.** Complimentary audits are capped by cohort and reporting period. Product access, if offered, follows the published or separately approved trial terms.
3. **No guarantee.** Roof AI does not promise recovered revenue, booked jobs, claim outcomes, emergency dispatch or a specific conversion rate.
4. **Fit protects both brands.** Contractors must have legitimate inbound demand, service capacity and an owner for follow-up. Roof AI may decline spam-heavy, out-of-scope or no-capacity accounts.
5. **Customer controls.** Each contractor approves its scripts, qualification rules, scheduling limits, escalation language and communication channels before activation.
6. **Consent and privacy.** The contractor remains responsible for having authority to provide call data. Only the minimum necessary data is used. Legal/compliance questions require appropriate counsel.
7. **Editorial independence.** RoofersCoffeeShop controls whether and how it covers the topic. Product access does not buy favorable coverage.
8. **Brand use by permission.** Neither party uses the other's logo, customer list, endorsements or results without written approval.
9. **Attribution window for evaluation.** Use a proposed 90-day introduction window for the pilot scorecard. Any future referral compensation requires a separate signed agreement defining eligibility, payment timing, refunds, taxes and exclusions.
10. **Future economics must preserve margin.** If the pilot earns expansion, model partner compensation against collected subscription revenue—not trials, audit requests or estimated pipeline. Recommended negotiating ceiling: no more than 20% of collected subscription revenue for the first three paid months, with no lifetime commission, unless unit economics support an exception.
11. **Easy stop.** Either party may stop the pilot at any time; active contractors receive a clear handoff and data/export treatment consistent with their agreement.
