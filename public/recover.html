Before you decide

Straight answers.

Is this a lead-generation service?

No. The focus is on responding to calls and opportunities your roofing business already receives. There is no promise of a set number of leads, appointments, or paid jobs.

Do I need to change my business number?

The goal is to preserve your existing public number where the phone provider and forwarding setup support it. Number routing and compatibility need to be checked during setup; this page does not change your phone service.

How much does it cost?

The advertised service is $299/month with a 7-day free trial. The current signup and checkout flow shows the applicable payment, tax, billing, and cancellation details. This assessment is free and does not create a subscription.

Will this replace everyone answering our phones?

No. Your team can continue answering first. The recovery workflow is for missed calls and the configured follow-up path. The exact handoff and calendar integrations are confirmed during setup.

Will you call or text me automatically?

No. This form requests email follow-up about your inquiry. It does not grant permission for marketing SMS or AI-generated calls. The optional plan email is a single requested message, not an automated nurture sequence.

What happens after I request a review?

Your request is stored in Roof AI’s review queue. An on-screen confirmation includes the request reference. A demonstration request is not a booked appointment; a time would need to be arranged and confirmed separately. You can watch the on-demand demo or explore signup immediately.
