How to run the audit

A free missed-call audit worksheet for roofing companies: score your own call log without sharing names or recordings.

  1. Choose a recent seven-day period, or 30 days for low volume.
  2. Count unique callers once; exclude spam, vendors and existing projects.
  3. Mark each caller's documented next step and owner, and whether business-hours, after-hours response or storm surge.
  4. Complete the worksheet, keeping repairs and replacements separate.
  5. Compare with your own previous period, not hypothetical samples; see documented follow-up.

Optional split: unique missed callers and those still awaiting a next step during business hours, after hours and a storm surge.

How to read your result

A gap is a caller with no documented next step, not a lost job. Your first audit is a baseline; the second shows whether the gap is shrinking. Keep signed jobs and collected revenue separate from modeled value.

Audit FAQs

What is a roofing missed-call audit?

A count of your own missed callers over a set period, checked for a documented next step.

Do I need to share my call log?

No. Aggregate counts suffice; names, numbers and recordings stay in your systems.

How is this different from a missed-call calculator?

A calculator estimates from inputs; the worksheet counts what your log shows.
